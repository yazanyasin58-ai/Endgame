## Google Business Profile set

Twenty photographs taken from the **"By owner"** tab of the company's Google
Maps listing. That tab is the one that matters: Google separates uploads by the
business from uploads by visitors, and only the former are the company's to
publish. Originals are in `google/`; the web derivatives are
`public/img/project-*.webp`, resampled to 1200px on the long edge, WebP q82.
