# Impeccable Audit — Interior Design Flooring

Date: 2026-10-17 · Target: whole site, 28 routes · Branch: claude/idf-homepage-five-directions-ycul73

Evidence: Chromium 1194 via Playwright against `astro preview` on :4360, desktop 1440x900
and phone 390x844 (emulated viewport, `isMobile`, `hasTouch`); carousel gestures driven by
CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, not synthesized `TouchEvent`. Contrast read from painted
pixels (screenshot -> canvas -> `getImageData`), because computed-style walk-ups invert the
answer on this site: `.hp-final` paints its ground with a `linear-gradient`, so every
ancestor reads transparent and falls through to `body`'s cream. Bundled detector run on all
28 routes.

Untested: H.264 playback. This Chromium build returns `""` for
`canPlayType('video/mp4; codecs="avc1.42E01E"')`, so `canplay` never fires and the scrub
hero never reports ready in this container. Download behaviour is measured; the scrub
itself is not.

## Implementation Integrity Verdict

**Pass.** This is a product-specific system, not an interchangeable template.

One drafting language runs through the whole site and is applied consistently: a 40px
blueprint grid at 7% gold alpha behind page heads (`.hp-pagehead::before`, radially masked),
corner-bracket crops on `article.hp-xgroup::after`, numbered section labels ("01 / Scope"),
and the `.dr` draft-rule component. These read as one decision about what a Class A
contractor's site looks like, not as scattered reflexes.

The code carries its own reasoning. `ScrubHero.astro` documents why the film is a Blob
rather than a streamed source, and the MIME trap that makes a type-less Blob fail with
`MEDIA_ELEMENT_ERROR` code 4 after a perfect download. `SiteNav.astro` documents why the
menu stays a `<details>` and then hand-builds everything a modal needs on top: `inert` on
the rest of the page, a wrapping focus trap, Escape, scrim-click, focus returned to the
toggle, and an `aria-label` that swaps to "Close menu". `home.css` carries a comment
naming the three form grounds, which is the comment that caught a scoping error earlier
in this project.

Token discipline is real: 825 `var(--)` uses against 30 raw hex literals, and 8 of those 30
are `#000`/`#fff` mask stencils inside `mask-image`, which are alpha channels, not colours.

Two detector findings are verified false positives and are not reported below:

- `low-contrast` on `a.hp-btn.hp-btn--sm` at "2.5:1" — the header's Free Estimate button.
  Painted pixels across its interior are 6,514 px of `rgb(200,162,74)` and 640 px of
  `rgb(20,17,13)`: **7.82:1**. The detector sampled across the pill's rounded, antialiased
  edge against the page ground.
- `codex-grid-background` attributed to `body` on 25 routes. No `repeating-linear-gradient`
  exists in any stylesheet and nothing matching paints on `body` or `html`. The real element
  is `.hp-pagehead::before`, a masked 7%-alpha motif, correctly gated behind
  `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

The honest marks against integrity: `background-clip: text` gradients on 8 elements, the
same gold radial spotlight glow on 6 section heads, and an error colour that drifted into
six spellings. Isolated, not systemic.

## Executive Summary

- Audit Health Score: **14/20** (Good — address weak dimensions)
- Issues: **0 P0, 4 P1, 5 P2, 3 P3**
- Top issues:
  1. The focus indicator is ground-blind. Gold `#e2c06b` measures 7.5–10.7:1 on dark
     grounds and **1.65:1** on paper. Every page's header sits on paper.
  2. Paper form fields remove the outline entirely and signal focus with a 1px gold
     border at **2.41:1**. This is the estimate and contact path.
  3. `/custom-homes/` dims step text to 0.4 and 0.6 opacity: **2.43:1** and **4.27:1**
     measured from pixels. Reduced motion removes the transition but not the dimming.
  4. Desktop downloads an **11.46 MB** hero film as a single Blob, with no Save-Data or
     `effectiveType` guard — the guard the 1.1 MB mobile film already has.
- Next steps: one ground-aware focus token fixes findings 1 and 2 together; the dimming
  floor is a two-line change; the Save-Data guard is one condition reusing `lean()`.

## Detailed Findings by Severity

### [P1] Focus ring invisible on every paper ground

- **Location**: `src/styles/global.css:104` — `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--gold-bright) }`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Impact**: Keyboard users lose their place on every page. The site header is paper on
  all 28 routes, so the logo, phone link, Free Estimate button and menu toggle all take a
  ring that is effectively invisible. Measured from painted pixels: ring `rgb(226,192,107)`
  on ground `rgb(251,248,242)` = **1.65:1**. The same ring on dark grounds measures
  10.74:1 (promo bar), 8.09:1 (hero button), 7.51:1 (`a.hp-call`), so the colour is right
  for half the site and wrong for the other half.
- **WCAG**: 2.2 SC 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (3:1)
- **Recommendation**: Make the ring ground-aware the way `--ef-err` already is. Define
  `--focus-ring` defaulting to a dark value for paper (`--gold-deep` `#856719` measures
  4.1:1 on `rgb(251,248,242)`), then override to `--gold-bright` under the dark-ground
  selectors (`.hp-final`, `.sg`, `.promo`, the hero). Paper is the right default: most
  surfaces are paper.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Paper form fields suppress the focus outline

- **Location**: `src/styles/home.css:431` (`outline: none`), `:959`, `:1352`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Impact**: On `/estimate/` and `/contact/` the focused field has no outline at all. The
  only indicator is the 1px border changing from `rgba(20,17,13,0.18)` to `--gold`
  `rgb(200,162,74)`, measured at **2.41:1** against the white field. A keyboard user
  filling the lead form cannot reliably tell which field has focus — on the two pages the
  business depends on. The dark homepage form does not have this problem: the same gold on
  the near-black field has ample contrast.
- **WCAG**: 2.2 SC 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (3:1)
- **Recommendation**: Keep the border tint as the soft cue, but stop removing the outline.
  Restore a 2px `--focus-ring` with `outline-offset: 2px` on `.ef-input:focus-visible` so
  the paper forms inherit the same indicator as everything else.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Step story text dimmed below readable contrast

- **Location**: `src/styles/pages.css:1069-1071`, inside `@media (min-width: 900px)`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Impact**: `/custom-homes/` dims process steps by scroll position. Measured from painted
  pixels at 1440x900: upcoming steps `rgb(164,161,155)` on `rgb(251,248,242)` = **2.43:1**;
  past steps `rgb(121,118,113)` = **4.27:1**. Both are 16px body paragraphs describing the
  build process. The reduced-motion block at `:1099` removes the *transition* but leaves
  the opacity, so a reduced-motion visitor gets the same unreadable text, snapping instead
  of fading.
- **WCAG**: 2.2 SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) — 4.5:1
- **Recommendation**: Raise the floor. `0.4` -> `0.72` and `0.6` -> `0.82` keeps the
  progressive emphasis while clearing 4.5:1 (the full-strength text measures 15.31:1, so
  there is plenty of headroom). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, drop the dimming
  entirely and hold every step at opacity 1.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Desktop hero downloads 11.46 MB with no Save-Data guard

- **Location**: `src/components/ScrubHero.astro:352-390` (`startBlobFetch` / `loadHeroBlob`)
- **Category**: Performance
- **Impact**: Measured: `hero-scrub.mp4` is 11,464 KB and arrives as one `fetch` whose
  stream is buffered into a Blob; `video.src` is only set after the last chunk, so the hero
  cannot respond to scroll until the whole file lands. Download completes at +11.8s on a
  9 Mbps connection and takes roughly a minute at 1.6 Mbps. The mobile path guards itself —
  `lean()` at `:461` checks `saveData` and `effectiveType` and is consulted at `:503` for
  the 1.1 MB film — but `lean()` is never consulted on the desktop path. A laptop tethered
  to a phone, on a metered hotspot, or with Save-Data on, takes the full 11.46 MB with no
  way out.
  What works: DCL is 648ms, the `h1` and the whole page below render immediately, scrolling
  to 4000px works while the film downloads, there is an honest progress ring reading real
  bytes, a 20s stall watchdog, and a graceful "Scroll" cue on failure. The page is usable
  throughout; the cost is bandwidth, not a blocked visitor.
- **Recommendation**: Gate `startBlobFetch` on `!lean()` — the function already exists.
  When lean, hold the poster and show the static hero, which is the existing fallback. Worth
  considering separately: `hero-scrub.mp4` has not been re-encoded since 26 September while
  `hero-mobile.mp4` was reprocessed on 15 October down to 1.1 MB.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P2] Placeholder text below AA

- **Location**: `#est-address`, `#ct-address`, `#lyh-address`, `#lyh-details`; no
  `::placeholder` rule exists in any stylesheet, so these are browser default grey
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Impact**: "City or ZIP at minimum" renders `#8a8886` on `#ffffff` = **3.5:1**. The
  placeholder carries real instruction here, not decoration, so it needs to be readable.
  `#hp-address` on the dark form measures 4.3:1 — closer, still short.
- **WCAG**: 2.2 SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)
- **Recommendation**: Add a `::placeholder` rule using an existing token. `--gray` or a
  `#6b6660`-class neutral clears 4.5:1 on white.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Menu index links sit under 3:1 at rest

- **Location**: `src/styles/nav.css:318`, `:334`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Impact**: Opening the full-screen menu sets one group active and leaves the rest at
  `opacity: 0.35`. Cream `#f6f1e7` on the panel's `rgb(23,38,61)` is 13.51:1 at full
  strength; composited at 0.35 it is **2.91:1**. At 37.44px these count as large text, so
  the bar is 3:1 — it misses by 0.09. The `:focus-within` variant at `:334` applies the
  same dim to keyboard users.
- **WCAG**: 2.2 SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), large-text threshold
- **Recommendation**: Raise the resting opacity from `0.35` to `0.40`. That clears 3:1 with
  margin and the emphasis effect survives intact.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] One 136 KB stylesheet ships to all 28 pages

- **Location**: `dist/_astro/about.Q_S5eoG6.css` — 136,152 bytes raw, **25,887 bytes
  gzipped**, linked by 28 of 28 built pages
- **Category**: Performance
- **Impact**: Every route, including `/sitemap/` and `/404/`, downloads and parses the CSS
  for every other route. It is render-blocking. 25.9 KB gzip is not fatal, but it is the
  single largest text asset on the lightest pages — `/estimate/` transfers 27 KB in total.
- **Recommendation**: Split the shared shell (tokens, layout, nav, footer, buttons) from
  the page-specific sheets, or let Astro scope per-route styles instead of concatenating
  the whole system into one bundle.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P2] Footer column headings are h3 with no h2 above them

- **Location**: `src/components/SiteFooter.astro:27,29,42,50,52,65`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Impact**: Six `<h3 class="foot-head">` headings with no `h2` parent. On most pages the
  main content supplies `h2`s first so the gap is hidden, but `/estimate/` has only an `h1`
  in its main content, so its outline reads h1 -> h3 -> h3 -> h3 -> h3 -> h3 -> h3. A
  screen-reader user navigating by heading level is told there is a missing level.
- **WCAG**: SC 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (heading hierarchy)
- **Recommendation**: Promote `.foot-head` to `h2`. Visual size is set by the class, so
  nothing changes on screen.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Google Fonts is render-blocking with no self-hosted fallback

- **Location**: `src/layouts/Page.astro:38`, preconnects at `src/layouts/Base.astro:69-70`
- **Category**: Performance
- **Impact**: A second render-blocking stylesheet on a third-party origin, requesting four
  Fraunces axes plus IBM Plex Mono and three Public Sans weights. The preconnects and
  `display=swap` are correct and do real work, but if `fonts.googleapis.com` is slow or
  blocked the page waits on it, and there is no local `@font-face` to fall back to.
- **Recommendation**: Self-host the subset actually used. It removes a third-party
  round-trip on every page and makes the typography independent of a network Google
  controls.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P3] The error colour exists in six spellings and no token

- **Location**: `global.css:314,317`; `home.css:499,511,969,1365,1726`
- **Category**: Theming
- **Impact**: `#a84420`, `#99301a`, `#E08A6B` (x2), `#A84420` (x2, differing case), and
  `#a8341f` are conceptually two colours: error-on-paper and error-on-dark. Six literals
  with inconsistent casing is how the next person introduces a seventh.
- **Recommendation**: Promote to `--err`, `--err-rule`, and their dark-ground overrides,
  following the `--ef-err` pattern already established in the form work.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable extract`

### [P3] Measures run long on desktop

- **Location**: 24 instances across `/about/`, `/investors/`, `/privacy/`, `/terms/`,
  `/real-estate/`; e.g. `div.inv-intro > p:nth-of-type(1)` at ~108 characters per line
- **Category**: Responsive Design
- **Impact**: 95–108 characters per rendered line at 1280px on the long-form pages, against
  a 65–80 comfortable range. Mobile is clean — zero paragraphs over 85ch at 390px — so this
  is desktop-only, and it lands hardest on the pages people actually read top to bottom.
- **Recommendation**: A `max-width: 68ch` on the prose containers for these pages. The
  pattern already exists: `.ch-step-detail` sets `max-width: 36ch`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

### [P3] Reflex decoration

- **Location**: `background-clip: text` gradients on 8 elements (the five `.hp-figure-value`
  stats, `p.hp-wedge-tagline`, `p.pl-foot-mark`); the same gold radial spotlight glow on 6
  section heads (`#why-one-team`, `.ct-details`, `.hp-est-intro`, `.svc-split`, two
  `.hp-section`)
- **Category**: Implementation Integrity
- **Impact**: Both are saturated moves the detector flags on sight. Gradient text also
  makes contrast unmeasurable per-glyph, which matters more on stat figures than on a
  tagline. Six instances of one glow is the point where a signature becomes a default.
- **Recommendation**: Keep the glow where it earns the composition, drop it where it is
  filling space. Solid `--gold` on the stat figures would read stronger than the gradient.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

1. **Contrast decisions are ground-blind; structural decisions are not.** The same root
   cause produces the focus ring failure, the form field failure, and the paper/dark split
   generally: a colour is chosen for one ground and applied to both. The codebase already
   solved this once — `--ef-err` defaults to paper and is overridden under `.hp-final` —
   and a comment in `home.css` names the three grounds explicitly. The pattern exists; it
   has not been applied to the focus system.
2. **Opacity is used as an emphasis tool without a readability floor.** The step story
   (0.4 / 0.6) and the menu index (0.35) both dim real text below AA. Neither is restored
   under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
3. **Mobile got a second pass that desktop did not.** `hero-mobile.mp4` was re-encoded to
   1.1 MB on 15 October and is guarded by `lean()`; `hero-scrub.mp4` is unchanged from 26
   September at 11.46 MB and is guarded by nothing.
4. **Carried forward, not re-litigated:** 85 instances of functional text below 11px
   (9px logo lockup, 10px draft labels and gallery tags) and 9 distinct controls under
   44x44 on a 390px phone (`button.promo-close` 21x22, `a.hp-call` 128x24,
   `button.rc-more` 72x25). Both were already on the list before this audit.

## Positive Findings

- **Alt text is complete.** 294 `<img>` tags across 28 pages: 0 missing the attribute, 206
  intentionally `alt=""` for decoration, 88 with genuine descriptions. No filename dumps,
  no "image of".
- **The menu is a textbook modal.** Click opens, click closes, Escape closes, scrim closes,
  focus moves to the first link on open and returns to the toggle on close, the rest of the
  page goes `inert`, `aria-label` swaps to "Close menu", and the trap wraps cleanly at 16
  stops with 0 escapes in 26 tab presses. Built by hand on a `<details>`, with a comment
  explaining why.
- **Every focus stop has a ring.** 45 of 45 elements walked carry a 2px outline — the
  colour is the problem, never its absence.
- **Tab order starts where it should.** First stop on load is `a.skip-link` "Skip to
  content".
- **CLS is 0** on `/`, `/projects/`, `/services/kitchen-remodeling/` and `/estimate/` at
  390px, despite 101 of 294 `<img>` tags omitting `width`/`height` — the CSS containers
  hold their own space.
- **Reduced motion is handled per component**, not with a global `0.01ms` kill: 43
  references, document height 8,960px vs 14,574px default, 0 sections left faded out, 0
  elements still animating.
- **Every scroll and resize listener is passive and rAF-coalesced.**
- **The carousel works under real touch.** CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`: swipe moves it
  113 -> 292, vertical page scroll is not swallowed (4657 -> 4858), arrows work (292 -> 607).
- **200% text scaling passes** on `/`, `/estimate/`, `/faq/` and `/services/kitchen-remodeling/`
  with no horizontal overflow, and **320px reflow passes** on `/`, `/estimate/` and
  `/projects/`.
- **13 KB of JavaScript, total, across the whole site.** Three runtime dependencies.
- **Mobile is lean and fast**: 1,425 KB total on the homepage, LCP 540ms, 21 requests;
  `/estimate/` transfers 27 KB.
- **Zero visible controls without an accessible name**, including every icon-only button
  (`button.promo-close` -> "Dismiss offer", the four `button.rc-arrow` -> "Previous reviews",
  "More reviews", "Previous project", "Next project").

## Recommended Actions

1. **[P1] `/impeccable harden`**: One ground-aware `--focus-ring` token fixes the 1.65:1
   header ring and the 2.41:1 form field together; raise the step-story opacity floors
   (0.4 -> 0.72, 0.6 -> 0.82, no dimming under reduced motion); add a `::placeholder` rule;
   nudge the menu index to 0.40; promote `.foot-head` to `h2`.
2. **[P1] `/impeccable optimize`**: Gate `startBlobFetch` on the existing `lean()` so
   Save-Data and 2G desktops are not sent 11.46 MB; re-encode `hero-scrub.mp4` the way
   `hero-mobile.mp4` was; split the 136 KB shared stylesheet; self-host the font subset.
3. **[P3] `/impeccable typeset`**: Cap the measure at ~68ch on the long-form pages.
4. **[P3] `/impeccable extract`**: Pull the six error-colour literals into tokens.
5. **[P3] `/impeccable quieter`**: Thin out the six radial glows and the eight gradient-text
   elements.
6. **`/impeccable polish`**: Final pass once the above land.
